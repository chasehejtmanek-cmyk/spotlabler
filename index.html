How it works

  1. Tap the spot — right on any wall, rail, ledge, or gap you could do parkour on. A square drops on it and you jump to the next view.
  2. Want a custom size? click and drag to draw a box of any size around the spot. The orange outline previews it; release to place.
  3. Nothing there, or blocked? hit No visible spot (or press N).
  4. Each location shows 4 views (N · E · S · W). Misclicked? Back (B).
